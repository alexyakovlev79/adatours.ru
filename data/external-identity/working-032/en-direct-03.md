Highlands with turtles!
Famous for its calm, clear lagoons and diverse marine life, it’s a perfect spot for snorkeling alongside gentle sea turtles, marine iguanas, and playful rays. As you stroll along the serene shoreline, you're enveloped by the beauty of nature, from the vibrant birdlife to the lush mangroves that frame this untouched coastal haven.

Impressions
Tortuga Bay: It is among the 25 best beaches in the world. We will take a walk of approximately 45 minutes to the beach. It is a beach of approximately 2, white sand formed by fish bones and white coral; considered a sanctuary for iguanas, sea turtles and other species where you can swim and perform activities such as swimming and beach games. Highlands: Natural habitat of approximately 8,000 giant tortoises of Santa Cruz Island. They prefer the highlands of the island, where the climate is cooler, more humid and they will find more food and rainwater. Lava tunnels are majestic geological formations through which hot lava flowed. We will take a tour of the interior together with a naturalist guide. The Twin Craters, is a pair of sinkholes, one on each side of the road, which is why it is called this way. Here we have the opportunity to see the famous Escalecia forest, an endemic plant of the Galapagos Islands and one of the favorite habitats of the finches. The main activities are walking, photography and filming, it is recommended to wear sports shoes.
