North Seymour Island
Its rocky landscape is alive with the vibrant dance of blue-footed boobies, while the courtship rituals of magnificent frigatebirds fill the skies. Along the rugged trails, you'll encounter land iguanas basking in the sun and sea lions lounging lazily by the shore. The island’s raw, untamed beauty offers an intimate glimpse into one of the world’s most unique ecosystems.

Impressions
This excursion day will offer you an amazing opportunity to visit North Seymour Island and the Bachas beaches. We will travel by bus to our yacht in the Itabaca Channel, followed by a one-hour sail to North Seymour Island.
Disembarkation will take place on the rocky shore near a small forest of gray and silver Palo Santo trees, which are mostly leafless most of the year.
